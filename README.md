# Minigame 1
## Devlog
I think of a Scene as a canvas that holds a whole composition. GameObjects are the individual things in the picture, such as platforms, coins, and spikes. Components are like the details that give each painted object qualities, but in Unity they also control how objects behave. These objects can respond to the player. Copying a whole spike object helped me understand that the appearance (the models) must work with the components to make the player aware of the danger and kill the player. I also learned to adjust mouse sensitivity through Rotation Speed in the FirstPersonController component.

For my level, I wanted players to follow the coins and jump toward the highest point while avoiding spikes. Most coins guide the player to the top. However, I also put a coin that is simply there to collect. From an artist‘s perspective, placing the coins feels like choosing where I want the viewer to look next. In this game, I am also guiding where the player moves.
